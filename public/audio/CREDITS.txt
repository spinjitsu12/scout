# SCOUT audio credits
All recordings remain bundled for offline play.

SCOUT 3.0.1 uses warm piano, acoustic instruments, clarinet and restrained strings to accompany its stylized regional world. Campuses and villages favor lighter arrangements; coastlines and evening highways favor spacious strings; late-night walks favor quieter piano and clarinet. Complete recordings continue across doors and town boundaries. Short stretches of world sound separate arrangements, and conversations temporarily lower the music.

The original Ogg field recording is distributed without editing. SCOUT plays short excerpts with gentle fades and long quiet gaps during clear daylight, with a lower level inside the car. Foreground park birds fade out indoors, at night, in rain and on the interstate. Footstep recordings follow the actual floor material: wood, carpet, concrete or grass.

The generic phone vibration sample and engine, tire, wind, room, distant traffic and rain textures are original procedural SCOUT sound design. Motor and road textures respond continuously to movement; distant traffic passes quietly through the stereo field, while coastal air swells slowly. Recruitment and progression have no reward jingles. Original SCOUT audio remains part of the game's copyright, identified in the asset manifest as LicenseRef-SCOUT-Original.
