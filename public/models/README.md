# Original SCOUT 3D assets

All models in this folder are original geometry created for SCOUT. No third-party art is included. The models may be used, edited, and redistributed with this project by its owner.

The runtime builds these same assets from src/lib/immersive-assets.ts so labels and local material textures remain editable. These binary glTF copies can be opened in Blender and other glTF editors. No online asset download is needed during play.

Units: metres. Up: +Y. Vehicle and people face -Z. The compact has a real driver eye position (-0.43, 1.38, 0.25), articulated wheels and steering wheel. People have named articulated arm groups and a held phone.

Included: Juniper 82 compact car; 16 scouts with distinct skin, outfits, hair, and accessories; furnished laptop desk; sofa; bookshelf; bed; cafe table; park bench; street lamp; office chair; potted plant.
